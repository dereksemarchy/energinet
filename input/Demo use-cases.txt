and which depend on third-party products or future roadmap items.[10:30 AM]Use case: Creating and publishing a new data product

Objective:
The purpose of this use case is to demonstrate how a data owner or data steward can create,
govern, publish, and maintain a new data product based on existing operational data.
Scenario:

A data owner wants to establish a new data product using data already available within, or accessible to, the proposed solution.

The data product could, for example, be based on:
• SCADA measurements
• PMU or power-quality measurements
• Substation or sensor data
• Asset information

Metadata or contextual information from an authoritative network model

The resulting data product is intended for use by one or more consumers, such as control centre applications, operational planning, analytics, or Asset Management.
